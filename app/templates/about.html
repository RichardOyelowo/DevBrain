About

Active recall for people who write software.

DevBrain tests what you think you know, then keeps the miss as a reviewable record. No trivia feed. Owned questions, categories, and language filters.

Who it is for

Students, working developers, and anyone who learns better by answering than by rereading notes.

How practice is structured

A category is the skill. A language is optional syntax for code-reading. Difficulty and length stay in your control.

After each quiz

You get a score, a grade, the selected vs correct option, an explanation, and a dashboard signal for the next session.

Why active recall

Tutorials feel productive. Retrieval under a small amount of pressure shows what actually stuck.
